About Me

I'm a Research Fellow at Microsoft Research India, currently working with Dr. Akshay Nambi. My current work focuses on on-policy distillation and self-distillation. Specifically, I am looking at how these techniques can be used for better credit assignment and reward shaping in RLVR settings for reasoning and agentic tasks. I am also interested in robustness, privacy and alignment with LLMs.

I will be applying for PhD positions in the upcoming cycle for sessions starting in Fall 2027. Please feel free to contact me if you share research interests. I am always open to collaborations!
